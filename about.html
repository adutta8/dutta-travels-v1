About Dutta Travels

A family-run bus rental service based in Habra, West Bengal

Our Story

Dutta Travels is based in Habra, West Bengal, and has been helping groups travel comfortably across India for school trips, pilgrimages, weddings, corporate outings and family tours. We operate a single, carefully maintained 55-seater bus — which means every booking gets our undivided attention, from the first enquiry to the final drop-off.

While we're proud of our roots in West Bengal, our real specialty is planning trips to North India — from the hills of Darjeeling and Sikkim to Himachal Pradesh, Kashmir and Uttarakhand. We know these routes well and can help plan a smooth itinerary for long-distance charters.
